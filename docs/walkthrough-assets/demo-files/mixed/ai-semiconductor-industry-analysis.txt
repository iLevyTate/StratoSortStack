AI Semiconductor Industry Analysis — Market Outlook 2026–2031

Morgan Stanley Equity Research | Sector: Technology Hardware
Lead Analyst: Jennifer Park, CFA
Date: September 2026

INVESTMENT THESIS
The AI accelerator market remains in a multi-year build-out driven by
enterprise inference, sovereign AI infrastructure, and still-rising training
clusters. We project the total addressable market to grow from $96B (2025) to
$340B by 2031, a 23% CAGR from this year's base.

COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

NVIDIA (NVDA) — Dominant Incumbent
  Market share (DC GPU): 84% (2025)
  Key product: Blackwell successors in volume; CUDA still the default stack
  Moat: Software ecosystem, networking attach, CUDA installed base
  Risk: Customer ASIC development (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Meta)
  Rating: Overweight

AMD (AMD) — Credible Challenger
  Market share (DC GPU): 12% (2025)
  Key product: MI350-class parts in hyperscaler production
  Opportunity: ROCm catching up; more design wins outside NVIDIA allocation
  Rating: Equal Weight

Intel (INTC) — Restructuring
  Market share (DC GPU): <3%
  Key product: Gaudi follow-ons (competitive TCO, still limited adoption)
  Challenge: Foundry mix, execution risk
  Rating: Underweight

Broadcom (AVGO) — Custom ASIC Leader
  AI ASIC revenue: $6.8B run-rate (Google, Meta, others)
  Opportunity: Additional hyperscaler engagements still converting in 2026–27
  Rating: Overweight

SUPPLY CHAIN ANALYSIS

TSMC remains the critical bottleneck:
  - CoWoS advanced packaging capacity: ~70K wafers/month (mid-2026)
  - Expanding toward ~90K wafers/month by end of 2026
  - Leading-edge allocation for AI still takes a majority of relevant starts

HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) suppliers:
  SK Hynix: still the lead share in HBM3E / HBM4 ramp
  Samsung: qualified across more AMD and custom-ASIC programs
  Micron: share rising as HBM3E yields improve

RISKS TO THESIS
1. Export controls: Further US restrictions on China could still cut NVIDIA TAM.
2. Model scaling plateau: If scaling laws flatten, training CAPEX could
   decelerate faster than inference growth compensates.
3. Power constraints: 1 GW+ data centers still wait on utility-scale power;
   permitting delays can slip installations 12–18 months.

VALUATION SUMMARY
  NVDA: Overweight; inference mix is the swing factor into 2027.
  AMD:  Equal Weight; software gap is narrower than a year ago, not closed.
  AVGO: Overweight on custom ASIC backlog.
  INTC: Underweight until foundry and accelerator wins are both visible.
