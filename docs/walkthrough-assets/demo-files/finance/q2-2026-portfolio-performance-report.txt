COMMENTARY
The portfolio outperformed the benchmark by 112 basis points in Q2, driven
primarily by semiconductor and software names plus a modest overweight in
international developed equities. The late-April rate spike was managed
through our rebalancing band: we added to duration as yields peaked and
trimmed US large-cap after the May rally, which contributed about 35 bps of
alpha recovery into June.

Our alternatives sleeve posted a steady mark from Sequoia Growth Fund IV
(vintage 2022) after several portfolio companies reported stronger H1 bookings.
REITs lagged equities but still beat their sleeve benchmark as financing costs
eased into quarter-end.
