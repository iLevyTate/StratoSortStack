**Commercial use** (use for a company’s business, a paid product or service, or any purpose that is
not a permitted noncommercial purpose under that license) requires a separate paid license from
Kennedy Applied Sciences, Inc.

## What you get

A signed offline license key (`SSORT1-…`) that this app verifies on-device. No account server. Keys
can include an update window (`maintenanceUntil`) and seat count.

## How to buy

Email **contact@kennedyappliedsciences.com** with:

- Organization name
- Number of seats
- Whether you need a commercial license to the source, the packaged app, or both

## In the app

There is no trial and no locked feature. The app runs the same with or without a key; the free build
is not a license for commercial use. Commercial users paste a purchased `SSORT1-…` key into Settings
→ License, which then shows who it is licensed to and the update window. The same screen has a **Buy
a commercial license** button that opens the email above.
