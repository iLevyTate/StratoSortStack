# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want the StratoSort Core project — including the GitHub repository, its issue tracker, pull
requests, and any associated communication channels — to be a welcoming, professional, and
productive space for everyone who participates.

We pledge to make participation a respectful experience for all contributors and users, regardless
of background or experience.

## Our Standard

In short: be considerate, be constructive, and assume good intent.

Examples of behavior we expect:

- Use clear, professional language in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Take responsibility for your own mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.

Examples of behavior we will not tolerate:

- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Disclosing another person's private information without their permission.
- Conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reference

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its underlying standard. The full
text is published at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and is
incorporated here by reference. Where this document and the Contributor Covenant differ in
interpretation, the Contributor Covenant text is authoritative.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces. Examples include using an official project
e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed
representative.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers by email at
**contact@kennedyappliedsciences.com** (put "Code of Conduct" in the subject line). For matters that
also involve a security issue, a private GitHub Security Advisory on the repository is an acceptable
alternative.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers may apply any of the following responses, depending on severity and context:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining what was inappropriate and why.
2. **Warning** — a public or private warning that further violations will result in temporary or
   permanent restrictions on participation.
3. **Temporary Ban** — a defined-duration restriction on interaction in project spaces.
4. **Permanent Ban** — a permanent restriction on interaction in project spaces.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
